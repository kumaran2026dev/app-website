HydroFlow Support

HydroFlow: Water & Habit Tracker is an offline-first Android app for logging water intake, tracking habits, viewing progress, and scheduling local reminders.

Common help

  • Notifications not appearing: Make sure Android notification permission is enabled for HydroFlow and that battery/background restrictions are not blocking scheduled reminders.
  • Data: HydroFlow stores core tracking data locally on the device.
  • App reset: Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored HydroFlow data.
