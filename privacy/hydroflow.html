Privacy Policy — HydroFlow

App: HydroFlow: Water & Habit Tracker
Package: com.kumaran.hydroflow
Effective date: October 4, 2026

HydroFlow is designed as an offline-first water and habit tracking application. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

HydroFlow may store the information you enter into the app, including:

  • Water intake entries and timestamps
  • Daily water goal
  • Habit names, habit notes, and habit completion history
  • Profile preferences such as display name, avatar, and optional body weight
  • Reminder and notification preferences

This information is stored locally on your device using local app storage, including SQLite on Android. It is used to provide HydroFlow's tracking, history, statistics, and reminder features.

Data collection and sharing

The current version of HydroFlow does not require an account and does not upload your water or habit tracking information to a HydroFlow server. HydroFlow does not sell your personal information.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, HydroFlow may request Android notification permission and schedule local notifications on your device. Notification preferences are stored locally. You can disable notifications in HydroFlow or through Android system settings at any time.

Internet access

Core tracking features are designed to work offline. If future versions add online services, analytics, advertising, cloud backup, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced.

Data retention and deletion

Your HydroFlow tracking information remains on your device until you remove it through the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored HydroFlow data depending on Android device behavior and backup settings.

Children's privacy

HydroFlow is a general wellness tracking tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account or server.

Security

Because HydroFlow's core data is stored locally, access to that information also depends on the security of your Android device. Keep your device and operating system protected and up to date.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HydroFlow's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For questions about HydroFlow or this Privacy Policy, visit the HydroFlow Support page or the developer's GitHub profile at github.com/kumaran2026dev.
